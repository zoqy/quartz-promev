> **Everything here is invented.** This is fixture content for design work — the real vault

> real person, machine, or credential.

## Conventions

Every note carries `author`, `created`, `tags` and `description` in its frontmatter. Cross-links
use `[[wikilinks]]`, so the graph view is a real map rather than decoration. Consumed media
notes add a `curator` field, separating who *made* a thing from who *filed* it.

## Deliberately awkward

A few notes exist to stress the layout rather than to be read — an over-long title, a table
wider than a phone, an unbreakable string, a four-deep list. If the design survives
[[Notes/a-note-with-a-deliberately-long-title-that-should-wrap-awkwardly|those]], it survives
real notes.
