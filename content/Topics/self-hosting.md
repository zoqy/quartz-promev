# Self-Hosting

The appeal is control. The cost is that you are now the operations team.

## What it's good for

- Data that shouldn't live on someone else's computer
- Services that outlive whichever startup built them
- Learning how things work by having to fix them

## What it isn't

Free. The hardware is cheap; the attention is not. Every service is a thing that can break at
an inconvenient moment, and a thing that needs updating.
