# Note-Taking Systems

Most systems fail the same way: capture is easy, retrieval is not, and a note nobody re-reads
is a note that was never written.

## What seems to survive

1. Links over folders. A note's neighbours matter more than its location.
2. Writing for a future reader who has forgotten everything — which is to say, yourself.
3. Small notes. A long note is several notes that haven't been separated yet.

> The test of a system is whether you can find something you half-remember from a year ago.
